EVIDENCE: Parallel build jobs append to `names.log` at the same time and Design takes no lock, so lines from concurrent writers can interleave.
PROPOSAL: Take a lock around each append, or state the limit.

KIND: judgement

WHERE: Design

PROPOSAL: Remove the retry sampler.
